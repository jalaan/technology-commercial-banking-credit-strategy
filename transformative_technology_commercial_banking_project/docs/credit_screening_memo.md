# AetherGrid Systems — Credit Screening Memo

> **Status:** Independent portfolio simulation. Not a formal bank credit memorandum or approval.

## 1. Request / Relationship Context
AetherGrid Systems is modeled as an existing commercial-banking client seeking continued access to an illustrative **$50MM revolving credit facility** to support working-capital flexibility, acquisition optionality, and seasonal operating needs.

The purpose of this screening is to determine whether the financial profile is strong enough to justify continued underwriting work and relationship expansion.

## 2. Company Overview
AetherGrid is a fictional cloud / AI observability software provider serving enterprise technology customers. The model assumes:
- subscription-heavy recurring revenue;
- high gross margins;
- moderate R&D and sales investment;
- increasing international expansion;
- positive but still developing free-cash-flow generation.

## 3. Historical Performance

| $MM except ratios | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 180 | 218 | 255 |
| EBITDA | 10 | 22 | 33 |
| EBITDA Margin | 5.6% | 10.1% | 12.9% |
| Free Cash Flow | 3 | 10 | 19 |
| Debt | 100 | 95 | 80 |
| Cash | 28 | 32 | 48 |

### Interpretation
Revenue growth remains strong while profitability and cash generation improve. Debt declines and cash increases, reducing net leverage.

## 4. Credit Metrics
The model focuses on:
- net leverage;
- DSCR;
- current ratio;
- FCF conversion;
- cash balance;
- recurring-revenue retention.

The 2025 financial profile indicates manageable leverage, but debt-service capacity remains sensitive to slower growth and margin compression.

## 5. Base Case
The base case assumes:
- 2026E revenue growth of 17.6%;
- 2026E EBITDA margin of 15.0%;
- an illustrative all-in borrowing rate of 5.90%;
- continued debt amortization.

Base-case 2026E DSCR is approximately **2.41x**.

## 6. Downside Case
The downside case assumes:
- 8% 2026 growth;
- 11% EBITDA margin;
- 7.25% illustrative borrowing cost;
- continued scheduled amortization.

2026E downside DSCR falls to approximately **1.29x**. This is the key risk finding: the borrower remains above the project's illustrative 1.25x monitoring threshold, but with limited headroom.

## 7. Key Risks
1. Customer retention falls faster than modeled.
2. Growth slows while R&D and go-to-market costs remain sticky.
3. International expansion creates FX volatility and operating complexity.
4. A large acquisition or incremental term debt weakens the current deleveraging trend.
5. A sustained higher-rate environment compresses coverage.

## 8. Risk Mitigants
1. High recurring-revenue mix.
2. Strong gross margin.
3. Positive and improving free cash flow.
4. Net debt reduction.
5. Growing cash balances.
6. Diversified treasury and deposit relationship can improve visibility into liquidity.

## 9. Preliminary Recommendation
Proceed to deeper underwriting / relationship review for continued revolver availability, while:
- avoiding aggressive incremental term leverage;
- validating customer concentration and ARR quality;
- monitoring downside DSCR;
- deepening operating deposits and treasury visibility;
- reviewing international FX exposures.

This conclusion is a **screening recommendation**, not a credit approval.
