# Case Interview Questions

Use these questions to practice defending the project.

## Financial Analysis
1. What are the three most important drivers of AetherGrid's forecast?
2. Why does EBITDA matter for credit but not equal cash flow?
3. What causes FCF to differ from EBITDA?
4. Why do you monitor both gross leverage and net leverage?
5. What would happen if working capital consumed more cash than expected?

## Credit
6. Why did you use DSCR?
7. Why is the downside case more important than the upside case?
8. What would make you decline or reduce the facility?
9. What additional information would you request before formal underwriting?
10. Why did you recommend against aggressive incremental term leverage?

## Technology
11. Why are NRR and ARR relevant to a software borrower?
12. How could customer concentration change the credit view?
13. How does slower SaaS growth affect debt-service capacity?
14. Why might a high-growth AI company still be a weak credit?

## Treasury
15. What treasury opportunities did you identify?
16. Why does operating-deposit penetration matter to the bank?
17. How would increasing international revenue change the client conversation?
18. When would you involve an FX specialist?
19. What data would you request to understand payment flows?

## Relationship Management
20. What should the Relationship Manager ask the CFO?
21. Which product partner should be brought in first and why?
22. How would you prioritize this client against the rest of the portfolio?
23. What would you monitor quarterly?
24. What cross-sell would you avoid because it does not fit the client?

## Methodology
25. Why is your score not a bank risk rating?
26. Which assumptions are most sensitive?
27. What are the limitations of synthetic data?
28. How would the analysis change with audited statements?
29. What would you automate if this were a real portfolio?
30. How would you improve the project with actual public-company data?
