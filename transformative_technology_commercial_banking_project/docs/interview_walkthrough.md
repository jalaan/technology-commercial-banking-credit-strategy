# Interview Walkthrough

## 30-Second Overview
“I wanted a project that went beyond valuation and actually mirrored a technology commercial-banking associate’s work. I created a synthetic portfolio of 12 middle-market technology companies, scored them on credit quality and relationship opportunity, then selected a flagship SaaS client for a full relationship review. I built the financial model, analyzed liquidity and debt service, stress-tested the borrower, mapped treasury and FX opportunities, and summarized the recommendation in an executive deck.”

## 2-Minute Walkthrough

### 1. Portfolio Prioritization
“I started at the coverage-team level. I did not assume the largest or fastest-growing company was automatically the best opportunity. I separated financial strength from commercial opportunity and built a weighted coverage-priority score.”

### 2. Financial Statement Analysis
“For the flagship company, I built historical and forecast income-statement, cash-flow, and balance-sheet metrics. I focused on revenue durability, margin expansion, cash conversion, leverage, liquidity, and recurring-revenue KPIs.”

### 3. Credit Analysis
“I calculated net leverage and debt-service coverage and then stressed both operating performance and borrowing costs. The most important finding was that the downside case remained above the project’s 1.25x monitoring threshold, but with limited cushion.”

### 4. Treasury / FX
“I then treated the client as a relationship rather than only a borrower. I analyzed collections, payments, operating cash, and international exposure and identified opportunities in receivables, liquidity, payments, and FX.”

### 5. Executive Recommendation
“My recommendation was not simply ‘lend more.’ I recommended maintaining revolver flexibility, limiting incremental term leverage, and deepening treasury and FX penetration while monitoring customer retention and downside cash flow.”

## Questions You Should Be Ready to Answer

### Why did you use a synthetic dataset?
To demonstrate the process without misrepresenting access to confidential bank or client information.

### Why is the credit score not a bank rating?
Because internal risk ratings are proprietary and institution-specific. The project uses a transparent screening model only.

### What would you add in a real underwriting process?
Detailed debt schedule, collateral, customer concentration, legal structure, guarantors, covenant package, management diligence, tax returns / audited statements, borrowing-base detail where relevant, and formal bank risk-rating methodology.

### What does this demonstrate?
Financial statement analysis, cash-flow modeling, commercial judgment, technology-sector understanding, client prioritization, executive communication, treasury awareness, and cross-functional thinking.
