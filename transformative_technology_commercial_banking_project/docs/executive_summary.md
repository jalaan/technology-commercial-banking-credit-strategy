# Executive Summary

## Purpose
Demonstrate an end-to-end technology commercial-banking coverage workflow using synthetic middle-market client data.

## Decision
AetherGrid Systems should remain an active relationship-development client. The simulated analysis supports maintaining revolving-credit availability while prioritizing treasury and FX penetration rather than adding aggressive term leverage.

## Financial Profile
- 2025 revenue: $255MM
- 2025 EBITDA: $33MM
- 2025 EBITDA margin: 12.9%
- 2025 free cash flow: approximately $19MM
- 2025 debt: $80MM
- 2025 cash: $48MM
- 2025 net leverage: approximately 1.0x
- 2026E base-case DSCR: 2.41x
- 2026E downside DSCR: 1.29x

## Relationship Opportunity
AetherGrid's expansion into international markets and rising payment volumes create opportunities across:
1. receivables and ACH;
2. operating liquidity;
3. global payment controls;
4. FX risk management;
5. revolving-credit support;
6. future capital-markets referrals as scale and strategic needs evolve.

## Primary Risks
- growth moderation;
- SaaS retention compression;
- fixed-cost investment ahead of revenue;
- increasing international complexity;
- downside debt-service cushion narrowing toward 1.25x.

## Recommended Next Steps
- conduct CFO / Treasurer relationship review;
- validate 2026 operating plan and cash-flow assumptions;
- review customer concentration and renewal schedule;
- perform formal FX exposure mapping;
- analyze treasury architecture and idle cash;
- maintain quarterly downside monitoring.
