# AetherGrid Systems — Relationship Review

## Relationship Objective
Deepen the relationship from a credit-led engagement into a broader operating-banking relationship.

## Client Priorities
- fund growth without over-levering;
- preserve liquidity;
- automate collections and payments;
- manage international expansion;
- improve cash visibility;
- reduce operational friction as transaction volume grows.

## Current Analytical View
AetherGrid's balance sheet is improving, but the strongest commercial opportunity is not incremental leverage. The more attractive relationship path is to pair revolving-credit support with treasury, payments, liquidity, and FX solutions.

## Product Opportunity Map

| Need | Observed Driver | Potential Solution Area | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working-capital flexibility | Fast growth / uneven cash timing | Revolving credit | High |
| Receivables automation | Rising customer collections | ACH / receivables solutions | High |
| Liquidity management | Growing operating cash | Deposit / liquidity structure | High |
| International expansion | International revenue increasing | FX / global treasury | High |
| Payment controls | Growing vendor payments | Wires / payment controls | Medium |
| Expense management | Scaling workforce | Commercial card | Medium |
| Strategic financing | Future M&A / capital needs | Investment banking referral | Watch |

## Proposed Client Meeting Agenda
1. 2026 operating plan and growth outlook
2. Cash-flow and liquidity priorities
3. Customer-renewal / concentration review
4. Treasury architecture and payment flows
5. International expansion and FX exposures
6. Credit flexibility and acquisition optionality
7. Strategic-capital needs over the next 12–24 months

## Relationship Next Actions
- Relationship Manager: schedule CFO / Treasurer review.
- Credit partner: refresh underwriting inputs and downside sensitivities.
- Treasury partner: map collections, disbursements, deposits, and liquidity.
- FX specialist: assess currency exposures by market and settlement flow.
- Investment Banking: remain informed for future capital-raising or M&A needs.

## Success Metrics
- share of operating deposits;
- treasury-services penetration;
- recurring client-review cadence;
- credit utilization and profitability;
- cross-product referrals;
- retained / expanded relationship wallet.
