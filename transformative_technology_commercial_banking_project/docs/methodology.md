# Methodology

## Design Principle
The project separates three questions that are often confused:
1. Is the company financially strong?
2. Is the relationship commercially attractive?
3. Does the combined profile justify coverage attention?

## Portfolio Scoring

### Credit Score
A 0–100 screening score based on revenue growth, EBITDA margin, FCF margin, net leverage, liquidity, retention, and qualitative risk flags.

This is not a probability of default, internal risk rating, or regulatory score.

### Opportunity Score
A 0–100 relationship score using estimated wallet and international complexity.

### Coverage Priority
`65% Credit Score + 35% Opportunity Score`

This weighting intentionally prevents a large wallet from overwhelming weak credit quality.

## Flagship Forecast
Historical financial statements are synthetic. Forecasts use transparent assumptions for:
- revenue growth;
- operating margins;
- capex;
- working capital;
- taxes;
- debt amortization;
- interest rates.

## Debt-Service Coverage
`DSCR = Cash Available for Debt Service / (Interest + Scheduled Principal)`

Cash Available for Debt Service is approximated as:
`EBITDA - Capex - Cash Taxes`

The project uses an illustrative 1.25x monitoring threshold solely as a scenario-screening device.

## Interest Rate Context
The Federal Reserve maintained the federal funds target range at 3.50%–3.75% on July 29, 2026. The project therefore uses a 3.65% illustrative reference/base rate in the base case and adds a synthetic credit spread.

## Treasury Analysis
Treasury opportunity is inferred from:
- customer collections;
- vendor payments;
- wire volume;
- ACH volume;
- FX notional;
- average operating cash;
- international revenue.

No proprietary fee schedules or bank pricing are used.

## Limitations
- Synthetic companies and financial statements.
- Simplified tax and working-capital mechanics.
- No formal collateral analysis.
- No legal-document review.
- No internal bank risk rating.
- No actual pricing or profitability model.
- No confidential Bank of America process or policy.
