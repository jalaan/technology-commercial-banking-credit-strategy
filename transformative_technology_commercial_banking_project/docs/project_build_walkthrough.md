# Project Build Walkthrough — Start to Finish

## Phase 1 — Define the Banking Question
The project begins with a coverage question rather than a modeling question:

> Which technology clients and prospects deserve attention, what is their financial condition, and which banking solutions should the team bring to them?

That framing prevents the project from becoming a generic valuation exercise.

## Phase 2 — Build the Coverage Portfolio
Create a synthetic portfolio of technology and emerging-growth companies inside the middle-market revenue range.

For each company, capture:
- revenue;
- growth;
- EBITDA margin;
- FCF margin;
- cash;
- debt;
- current ratio;
- NRR;
- international exposure;
- risk flags;
- estimated relationship wallet.

## Phase 3 — Build the Credit Screen
Convert operating data into credit-oriented metrics:
- EBITDA;
- net debt;
- net leverage;
- liquidity;
- FCF margin;
- credit score.

Keep the screening score transparent and explicitly state that it is not an internal bank rating.

## Phase 4 — Build the Relationship Opportunity Screen
Evaluate:
- wallet potential;
- international complexity;
- treasury needs;
- existing client vs. prospect status.

Combine credit and commercial opportunity into a coverage-priority score.

## Phase 5 — Select a Flagship Client
Choose a company that is strong enough to be interesting but has enough risk to require judgment.

AetherGrid is intentionally not a perfect borrower. Its downside DSCR approaches the monitoring threshold, which creates a realistic decision.

## Phase 6 — Build Historical Financials
Construct:
- income statement;
- cash-flow metrics;
- selected balance-sheet metrics;
- SaaS operating KPIs.

## Phase 7 — Forecast the Business
Forecast 2026E–2028E using:
- revenue growth;
- gross-margin / expense assumptions;
- EBITDA margin;
- taxes;
- working capital;
- capex;
- debt amortization;
- interest expense.

## Phase 8 — Build Credit Metrics
Calculate:
- gross margin;
- EBITDA margin;
- FCF margin;
- current ratio;
- net leverage;
- DSCR.

## Phase 9 — Stress the Borrower
Create Base, Downside, and Upside scenarios.

The Downside case combines:
- slower revenue growth;
- lower EBITDA margins;
- higher borrowing costs.

The purpose is to find the point at which debt service becomes uncomfortable.

## Phase 10 — Analyze Treasury Flows
Review synthetic monthly:
- collections;
- vendor payments;
- wires;
- ACH;
- FX notional;
- operating cash.

Translate operating flows into product opportunities.

## Phase 11 — Build the Relationship Review
Summarize:
- financial condition;
- key risks;
- credit view;
- treasury opportunities;
- FX needs;
- meeting agenda;
- product-partner actions.

## Phase 12 — Build Executive Outputs
The final outputs are:
1. Excel model and dashboard;
2. credit screening memo;
3. relationship review;
4. executive deck;
5. portfolio visualizations;
6. interview walkthrough;
7. resume bullets.

## Phase 13 — Present the Recommendation
The recommendation should answer:
- lend / do not lend / continue diligence;
- what structure or limitation matters;
- what needs monitoring;
- what non-credit solutions should be introduced;
- what the next client meeting should accomplish.
